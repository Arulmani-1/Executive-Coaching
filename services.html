- Executive Coaching · Est. 2013

Three engagement shapes.
One standard of rigor.

Every client starts with a calibration conversation before we agree on scope — so what follows here is a starting point, not a fixed menu.

01 · Individual

1:1 Executive Coaching

A confidential, ongoing partnership with one leader focused on behavioral change and strategic decision making.

  • Confidential partnership
  • 360° calibration
  • Weekly / biweekly sessions
  • Direct line for time-sensitive decisions
  • Typical length: 6–12 months
Inquire
Best Fit For

Leaders facing a step-change

New CEOs, executives inheriting a much larger org, and leaders whose 360 feedback keeps circling the same unresolved issue.

A02 · Team

Leadership Team Coaching

For the leadership team that's technically aligned but not actually pulling together. We work with the intact team — usually 5 to 9 people — through structured sessions that surface what the quarterly offsite never quite gets to.

  • — Confidential 1:1 interviews with every team member
  • — A shared, named diagnosis presented back to the team
  • — Monthly facilitated sessions over 2-3 quarters
  • — Optional coaching for individual members alongside
Inquire
Best Fit For

Teams that are aligned on paper

Executive teams where the org chart says "we're one team" but the meetings say otherwise — competing agendas, unspoken rivalries, or a founder who hasn't let go.

03 · Transition

First 100 Days

Focused support for leaders stepping into a new mandate. The goal is to accelerate time to value while avoiding the common traps of early leadership transitions.

  • Transition coaching for senior leaders
  • Stakeholder mapping
  • Early win identification
  • Communication strategy
Inquire
Best Fit For

Executives entering a new role, mandate or organization.

Leaders taking on a significantly larger P&L, moving to a new company, or tasked with a turnaround where the runway is short.
