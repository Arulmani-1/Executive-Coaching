- The Long Version

From operations floor to boardroom, then to the chair across from it.

Renata spent eleven years inside operating companies — six of them as COO of a mid-market logistics firm — before turning to coaching full time. She trained through an ICF-accredited program, holds a PCC credential, and has since worked with more than 140 executives across logistics, technology, and private equity.

She still opens every engagement the same way: not with a framework, but with a question she asks the people around her client. What she hears in the answers usually sets the entire agenda.
Meridian was founded in 2013 on a simple bet , that most executive coaching is too polite to be useful, and that leaders who've earned a senior seat can handle a direct account of what isn't working.

Philosophy

Leadership isn't a personality. It's a set of habits under pressure.

We do not try to change who you are. We optimize how you operate when the stakes are highest, the timeline is shortest, and the margin for error is zero.

- How I Work

Four commitments every client can hold me to.

Direct over comfortable

If it needs saying, I'll say it plainly — with care, but without softening it past the point of usefulness.

Evidence over opinion

Feedback comes from structured conversations with people around you, not from my read of you alone.

Confidential, fully

What's said in session stays there. Sponsors get themes and progress — never transcripts or quotes.

A defined end point

Every engagement has a stated finish line. I'm not building a client for life — I'm building a leader who doesn't need me.
